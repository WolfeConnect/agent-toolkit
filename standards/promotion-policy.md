# Promotion Policy

The toolkit is a publisher-first portability layer. Work begins in a private
source repository or local experiment and is promoted here only when it is
generic, portable, documented, and independently testable.

## Required checks

- No secrets, personal continuity, household topology, production endpoints,
  customer data, or generated runtime state.
- A catalog record with a stable ID, supported environments, provenance, and
  validation command or fixture.
- Clear licensing and attribution for original or upstream material.
- A manual exception record for any asset with uncertain work-origin history.

## Manual exceptions

Generalizing an asset does not establish publication rights. An uncertain
candidate remains private until Brendan approves a documented exception that
states its origin, permitted scope, and review date.
